# Support

## Public repositories

For reproducible bugs or focused feature requests, use the issue templates in the repository that owns the code.

Public issue trackers are intended for the public tools and pipelines published in the `unicorn-who-dev` organization. Include the project version, environment and a minimal reproduction. Do not publish credentials, private media, prompts, datasets or generated user artefacts.

## Commercial and private-source products

DAWWW-CORE and UnicorSoundEngine / UWdeVST are commercial products with private source repositories. Use their dedicated product websites and support channels rather than opening issues against unrelated public tools:

- https://dawww-core-local.com/
- https://unicorsoundengine.com/

## Independent initiatives

Projects maintained independently from the Unicorn Who Dev product portfolio use their own organization, documentation and support channels.
